# Research Wiki — Receipts & Regimes

The public research record behind the **Receipts & Regimes** channel
([@receiptsandregimes](https://youtube.com/@receiptsandregimes)). This is a
work-in-public quant research wiki: every published claim gets recorded, every
question gets filed, and studies produce verdicts — SUPPORTED / REFUTED / MIXED
— with failures getting prime time.

**Every number has a source.** No courses. No chat rooms. No promises.

## What this is

- `claims/` — atomic, testable claims extracted from sources, each with a
  status lifecycle (`untested → supported | refuted | mixed | …`).
- `questions/` — the live open-question backlog. **Open Question** videos pose
  these; follow-up videos carry the verdicts back here.
- `concepts/`, `entities/`, `comparisons/`, `queries/` — the knowledge layer.
- `studies/` — our replication protocols and verdicts. Executable code lives in
  the companion dev repo, referenced by commit hash.
- `raw/` — citation cards for ingested sources (metadata + attributed
  facts/numbers + link). Third-party full texts are NOT republished here; they
  stay in a private provenance store, hash-anchored by each card.
- `SCHEMA.md` — the rules this brain runs on. `index.md` — the catalog.
  `log.md` — the audit trail of every action.

## How videos cite this wiki

The public site is **[wiki.receiptsandregimes.com](https://wiki.receiptsandregimes.com)**.
Video descriptions and on-screen citations reference pages by slug — e.g.
`qs-rotation-beats-both-buyhold` →
<https://wiki.receiptsandregimes.com/claims/qs-rotation-beats-both-buyhold>
(source: <https://github.com/receiptsandregimes/research-wiki/blob/main/claims/qs-rotation-beats-both-buyhold.md>).
**Once a video cites a slug, that slug never changes and the page never
disappears.** Pages carry a `videos:` frontmatter field linking them back to
the videos that cite them.

## Not investment advice

Educational commentary on published claims and backtests. Backtests are
hypothetical: they depend on the stated universe, period, and costs, and past
performance — real or simulated — does not predict future results. Nothing here
is a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Do your own research.
