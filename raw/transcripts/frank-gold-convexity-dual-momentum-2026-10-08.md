# Frank — gold convexity, rotation onsets, and dual momentum (paraphrase)

Recorded 2026-10-08 from a chat with Frank (quant-librarian session). Frank's

## Frank's positions (paraphrased)

1. He rode the 2024–26 gold move by trading the gold/SPX ratio-rotation
   family ([[gold-spx-rs-ratio-rotation]]).

2. He treats gold as call-option-like: long flat/chop stretches bleed a
   portfolio, but the bleed is tolerable and can turn profitable once (a) a
   momentum/trend filter suppresses the lost-decade chop and (b) the position
   is levered — this is his own live practice
   ([[gold-call-option-filtered-levered]]).

3. At the onset of capital-rotation events he expects gold AND the S&P 500 to
   rise together, not alternate. Examples he points to: the mid-2000s gold
   bull coexisting with an equity recovery, and the collapse of the London
   gold peg in 1968 with US equities at highs ([[rotation-onset-co-rally]]).

4. He frames (2)+(3) as his answer to [[does-the-cash-leg-earn-its-keep]] —
   the method is "adding a filter to other things" — and it feeds
   [[gold-trend-filter-post-2023]].

5. He expects this line to terminate in dual momentum, citing Gary
   Antonacci's well-known work and Jay Kaeppel, whom he believes speaks
   similarly about gold.
