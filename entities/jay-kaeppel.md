(seven consecutive profitable years, per his bio); co-developer of option
trading software; 25+ articles in *Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities*.
Known for sentiment-based gold work (the "K-Ratio", gold/XAU miner ratio) and
long-standing multi-year gold theses.
