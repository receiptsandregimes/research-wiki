# Gary Antonacci

Investment professional (50+ yrs per his site Optimal Momentum); author of
*Dual Momentum Investing* (2013). Coined/popularized **dual momentum** =
absolute + relative momentum ([[dual-momentum]]); known portfolios GEM (Global
Equities Momentum) and GAA; publishes updated backtests on his own site and
Medium.

## As a source

- Publishes rules and updated performance openly (rare among strategy
  proponents) — but self-published, on assets he sells models around. Treat
  numbers as his claims, not third-party evidence.
- Central claims relevant to us: relative momentum adds return but not
  drawdown protection; absolute momentum protects drawdown at some cost; dual
  combination synergizes (his GEM-vs-SPX figure: +440 bps/yr 1950+, vs +200
  relative-only, +90 absolute-only — his numbers, unverified).
- Our wiki's P1 question [[does-the-cash-leg-earn-its-keep]] is his
  absolute-momentum claim re-tested on gold/SPX specifically. His evidence is
  equities/bonds; gold's 1980–2000 regime is the harder test he hasn't run.
