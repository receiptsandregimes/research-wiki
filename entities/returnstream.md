# Returnstream

Educational website (Returnstream LLC, returnstream.com) selling rule-based market-timing
strategies via paid ebooks and backtest spreadsheets. Self-description: helping the
"investing public learn that it's possible to beat the stock market indexes over the long
term through active market timing and stock picking."

## Relevance as a source
- Business model: teasers with equity curves + headline stats; full rule sets and
  spreadsheets are paywalled. Treat published numbers as vendor marketing — verify
  independently before trusting.
- Strategy family covered here: [[gold-spx-switching-strategy]] (momentum rotation).
- Other strategies advertised on the site (not yet ingested): Relative Strength Strategy,
  Weekly Reversion Strategy, Gold Market Neutral Strategy, Soybean Oil Calendar Spread,
  Fidelity sector mutual funds vs ETFs comparison.

## Source-quality notes
- [[gold-spx-switching-outperforms-both-assets]] numbers come from their own backtest;
  no data source, costs, or engine disclosed.
- Articles are otherwise well-illustrated with long-horizon (1975+) charts.
